Frequently Asked Questions
How do I borrow a book?

Sign up for a student account, browse the catalog, and click "Borrow" on any available title. You can also visit the library desk.

Can I request a new book?

Yes! Log in to your student account and use the Wishlist Desk to submit a book request with your contact details. Admin will review it.

How are fines calculated?

A fine of ₹5/day is applied for every day a book is returned past its due date. You can view and pay fines through your dashboard.

What is a Digital Pass?

Your Digital Pass is a QR-code based library card stored in your student dashboard. Show it at the desk for quick check-in/out.

I'm not a student. Can I still use the library?

Visitors are welcome! Use this contact form to inquire about guest access, book availability, or visiting hours.
